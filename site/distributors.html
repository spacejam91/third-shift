Questions

What owners ask

Our customers send orders in every format. Can it cope?

Emails, PDFs and spreadsheets, yes. Anything it can’t read with confidence is held for a person with the original attached, rather than guessed at.

Do we have to change our item codes?

No. It works from your price list and past orders, and holds anything that doesn’t match for a person.

How long does a first build take?

First builds typically ship within a few weeks. We run it alongside your team until it’s trusted, then hand it over.
