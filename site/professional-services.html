What stays with you

Your judgment stays yours

  • Client advice

    Nothing the automation does is advice. It handles the admin around your work and passes anything that needs judgment to you.

  • Confidentiality

    Client files stay in the systems you already use, with the least access that does the job. Where an AI model reads a document, it’s a business-grade service that doesn’t train on your data.

  • What clients see

    Every message goes out under your firm’s name, in words you approved, and you can stop reminders for any client at any time.

  • Money

    No money is paid or sent without a person signing off, unless you decide otherwise.

Questions

What owners ask

We’re an accounting firm. Isn’t this what we do for clients?

You do the accounting. We automate the admin around it, like document chasing, onboarding and billing, so the firm’s own hours go further. If your practice software already chases documents, we leave that alone and work on what it doesn’t.

Will it send anything to clients without us?

Only the messages you’ve approved, like document reminders, and you can stop them for any client at any time. Anything new waits for you.

How long does a first build take?

First builds typically ship within a few weeks. We run it alongside your team until it’s trusted, then hand it over.
