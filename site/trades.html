Questions

What owners ask

We use job management software. Can you work with it?

Often, yes. If it can export or connect, we build around it rather than replacing it. If it already sends reminders or follow-ups, we leave those alone and work on what it doesn’t, like supplier invoices and job costs.

Do my crews have to change how they work?

As little as possible. We work from where hours already go where we can, like a shared sheet, an email or a photo of the paper timesheet. If anything has to change, we’ll say so on the call.

What does a build cost?

Every build has a fixed price, written on the one-page plan before any work starts, along with what it costs to run each month. Any software or AI accounts it uses are in your name and billed to you directly. You know both numbers before you commit.
