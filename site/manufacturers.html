What stays with you

The decisions stay on your side

  • Purchasing

    Reorder lists and purchase orders are prepared for a person to approve. Nothing is ordered on its own.

  • Money

    No money is paid or sent without a person signing off, unless you decide otherwise.

  • Exceptions

    Price changes, short shipments and anything outside your rules are held with the reason, not pushed through.

  • Access

    Read-only wherever possible, a separate login for each automation, and access you can revoke at any time.

Questions

What owners ask

We have an ERP. Would you replace it?

No. We build around the systems you run, reading from them and posting to them where they allow. If your ERP already handles reorder points, we leave that alone and work on what it doesn’t.

Does it touch the machines?

No. This is office work: purchasing, invoices, stock, hours and reports. The shop floor stays as it is.

What does a build cost?

Every build has a fixed price, written on the one-page plan before any work starts, along with what it costs to run each month. Any software or AI accounts it uses are in your name and billed to you directly. You know both numbers before you commit.
