Other things we build

Examples, not the limit

Each of these is built for one business, in the systems it already uses.

  • Inventory counts and reorder alerts

    Stock checked against your minimums every night, with a reorder list ready for whoever does the buying. Nothing is ordered without a person.

  • Scheduling, dispatch and reminders

    The day’s schedule sent to each crew with addresses and notes, and reminders to customers the day before.

  • Timesheets and payroll prep

    Hours collected from wherever they’re entered, checked for gaps and overtime, and ready for payroll. A person approves before anyone is paid.

  • Quotes and contracts from templates

    Quotes and contracts filled in from your templates and the customer’s details, ready for you to check and send.

  • New-customer onboarding

    Accounts set up in each system, welcome emails and forms sent, and nothing missed when a new customer signs.

  • Syncing apps that don’t talk

    Orders, contacts or invoices moved between the systems that should share them, instead of being typed in twice.

  • Dashboards and small internal tools

    A simple screen for the job that’s outgrown a spreadsheet: a job tracker, a request form, an approval queue.

  • Month-end checklists

    The close steps run in order, each one checked off and logged, and anything missing chased before it holds up the month.

How we build

The same rules on every build

  • People approve the money

    No money is paid or sent without a person signing off, unless you decide otherwise.

  • Your data stays in your systems

    We build inside the accounts you already own. Where an AI model reads a document, we use business-grade services that don’t train on your data.

  • The least access that does the job

    Read-only wherever possible, a separate login for each automation, and access you can revoke at any time.

  • Every run is logged

    A plain-English record of what ran, what it touched and what it held back.
