How we build

Built for businesses that handle money

Automations touch your invoices, your bank and your customers. So they’re built the way a careful finance team would want them.

  • People approve the money

    No money is paid or sent without a person signing off, unless you decide otherwise. New or changed bank details are always held for a call-back.

  • Your data stays in your systems

    We build inside the accounts you already own. Where an AI model reads a document, we use business-grade services that don’t train on your data.

  • The least access that does the job

    Read-only wherever possible, a separate login for each automation, and access you can revoke at any time without breaking anything else.

  • Every run is logged

    A plain-English record of what ran, what it touched and what it held back. If you ever need to know what happened on a Tuesday night, it’s written down.

Questions

What owners ask first

What does a build cost?

Every build has a fixed price, written on the one-page plan before any work starts, along with what it costs to run each month. Any software or AI accounts it uses are in your name and billed to you directly. You know both numbers before you commit.

Do we need new software?

Usually not. We connect the email, spreadsheets and accounting system you already use. If a new tool would genuinely help, we say so and why.

Can you build something that isn’t on this list?

Yes. Invoices, inquiries and reports are where most owners start, but every build is made to fit. If a job repeats and lives in email, spreadsheets or an app you log into, we can usually automate it. Tell us what it is and we’ll say honestly whether it’s worth building.

Will AI make mistakes with our numbers?

The AI reads and sorts; rules and checks do the math. Anything outside the rules, like an invoice that doesn’t match its purchase order, is held for a person. You see every exception.

What happens when something breaks?

Every automation is monitored and logs each run. When something fails or looks wrong, it stops and tells a person instead of guessing.

How long does a first build take?

First builds typically ship within a few weeks. We run it alongside your team until it’s trusted, then hand it over.

Do you only work in Kitchener-Waterloo?

No. We’re based in Kitchener-Waterloo and meet clients in person across Waterloo Region: Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge. Elsewhere in Ontario, we work by video call.
