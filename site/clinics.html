Questions

What owners ask

Do you work inside our clinic software?

Usually not. Most of what we’d automate is on the business side, around it. If a build needs the clinic software, we use what it allows, read-only wherever possible.

What about patient privacy?

Most of this work doesn’t need patient information. Anything that would is planned with you and your privacy officer first, and only goes ahead if it fits your clinic’s privacy policies.

What does a build cost?

Every build has a fixed price, written on the one-page plan before any work starts. You know the number before you commit.
