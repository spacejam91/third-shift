Privacy

What happens to what you send us

Last updated October 20, 2026

Third Shift is a small business in Kitchener-Waterloo, Ontario. This page covers the website at thirdshift.ca and anything you send us through it or by email. The short version: we collect what you give us so we can reply, we don’t sell it or track you, and you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • The contact form: your name, work email, company, phone number if you add one, your message and the time you sent it. It also records how you found us: the campaign tag on the link you followed (for example, one of our Instagram posts or emails) or the name of the site that linked you here.
  • Email: whatever is in a message you send to thirdshift.ca@gmail.com.
  • If we emailed you first: your name, business email, role and company, taken from public business sources such as your company’s website, and the emails between us. Every one of those emails says who we are and how to tell us to stop. When you do, we stop.

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

To reply to you, book a call if you want one and, if we end up working together, to run that work. We don’t sell, rent or share it with anyone for marketing, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

Where it’s kept

Form submissions and email are stored in a Google account used only for Third Shift (Google Sheets and Gmail). Google may store that data on servers outside Canada, where it is subject to the laws of those countries. Access is limited to the people who run Third Shift.

Services this site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it processes technical details of each visit, such as your IP address.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: if the contact form shows a bot check, Cloudflare reads signals from your browser to tell people from bots.
  • Google Apps Script receives the contact form and writes it to our sheet.

How long we keep it

If an inquiry or an email conversation doesn’t turn into work, we delete it within two years of our last contact. If you ask us to stop emailing you, we keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so it stays stopped. If we work together, we keep records for as long as the work and Canadian tax and business record rules require.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email thirdshift.ca@gmail.com and we’ll answer within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with how we handle it, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
