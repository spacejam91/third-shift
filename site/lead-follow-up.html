What it handles

From first message to booked call

Leads rarely go cold because the quote was wrong. They go cold because nobody got back to them.

  • Logging every inquiry

    Website forms and emails land in one place, your CRM or a shared spreadsheet, with who they are, what they asked and how they found you.

  • A reply within minutes

    The person hears back within minutes, day or night, in your words, with the next step or a link to book a call. Real questions are passed to you.

  • Quotes followed up

    Open quotes get a polite follow-up on the schedule you choose. The follow-ups stop as soon as the customer replies or books a call, and you can stop them for anyone at any time.

  • One record per person

    Someone who emails and then fills in the form is one contact, not two, and returning customers are matched to their history.

  • The morning list

    One email before your day starts: new inquiries, replies waiting, quotes still open, and who’s worth a phone call.

  • Where leads come from

    Each inquiry records where it came from, when that’s known, so over time you can see which posts, ads or referrals bring in inquiries.

How it works

Nothing waits until someone remembers

It runs all day and all night, and it knows when to step back.

  1. 1

    Captured

    An inquiry arrives by form or email and is logged with its details and where it came from.

  2. 2

    Answered

    A reply goes out within minutes, written with you ahead of time. Anything that needs a real answer is flagged for you.

  3. 3

    Followed up

    Open quotes get follow-ups on your schedule, spaced a few days apart.

  4. 4

    Stopped

    A reply, a booked call or a no-thanks stops the follow-ups for that person straight away. You can also stop them for anyone, any time.

  5. 5

    Summarized

    The morning email tells you who to call and what’s waiting.

Questions

What owners ask

Will customers know it’s automated?

The first reply says you’ve got their message and what happens next, the way a good front desk would. It doesn’t pretend to be a person in a conversation. Follow-ups go out under your name, in words you approved, and real questions come to you.

Do we need a CRM?

No. A shared Google Sheet or Excel file works to start. If you already use HubSpot or another CRM, we connect to that instead.

What about Canada’s anti-spam law?

Follow-ups only go to people who contacted you or asked you for a quote, and they finish well within the six months the law allows after an inquiry. Every message carries your business name, mailing address and contact details with a way to unsubscribe, and a request to stop is honoured straight away.

What does a build cost?

Every build has a fixed price, written on the one-page plan before any work starts, along with what it costs to run each month. Any software or AI accounts it uses are in your name and billed to you directly. You know both numbers before you commit.
