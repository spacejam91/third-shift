What it handles

The invoice work nobody misses

The steps an accounts payable clerk does by hand, done in the same order, with the same checks, every night.

  • Collecting invoices

    From the AP inbox, supplier emails and the vendor portals you already log into. Each one is kept with the original attached, so nobody has to ask where a number came from.

  • Reading and coding

    Supplier, invoice number, dates, line items and GST/HST are read off the page and coded to the right accounts, using rules taken from how your team codes them today.

  • Matching

    Each invoice is checked against its purchase order and, where you track it, what was received. Prices, quantities and totals have to agree within the tolerance you set.

  • Duplicates and bank details

    The same invoice arriving twice, from a second email or a resent PDF, is flagged and held before it’s posted. New or changed bank details are always held for a call-back.

  • Posting and the payment run

    Matched invoices are posted as bills with the PDF attached and lined up for payment by their due dates. A person reviews and approves each payment run.

  • Due dates and discounts

    Due dates and early-payment discounts are tracked, so the morning summary says what’s due this week and which discounts are still worth taking.

How it works

From the inbox to an approved payment run

The AI reads and sorts. Rules and checks do the math.

  1. 1

    Collected

    After hours, new invoices are gathered from the inbox and the portals, with the originals saved.

  2. 2

    Read and checked

    The details come off each invoice, the math and the GST/HST are checked, and it’s compared with everything already posted.

  3. 3

    Matched

    Each invoice is compared to its purchase order and receipt, where you use them. Inside your tolerance, it’s posted. Outside it, it’s held with the reason written next to it.

  4. 4

    Summarized

    Before your team starts, one email: what was posted, what’s waiting and why, what’s due this week.

  5. 5

    Approved by a person

    Your team clears the exceptions and approves the payment run. Nothing is paid without that sign-off.

What stays with a person

Built the way a careful AP team would want it

The checks are the ones a careful accounts payable team makes by hand, in the same order, on every invoice, every night.

  • Payments

    No money is paid or sent without a person signing off, unless you decide otherwise.

  • Exceptions

    An invoice that doesn’t match, a supplier it hasn’t seen before, or anything outside the rules waits for a person, with the reason.

  • Bank details

    New or changed supplier banking information is always held for a call-back to a number you already have.

  • Access

    Read-only access to the inbox wherever possible, a separate login in your accounting system, and access you can revoke at any time.

Questions

What owners ask

Do we need purchase orders?

No. Without them, each invoice is checked against what you’ve paid that supplier before and against your coding rules, and anything unusual waits for a person. Purchase orders make the matching stricter, and we’ll tell you whether they’re worth adding.

What happens to an invoice it can’t read?

It’s held for a person with the original attached, and the morning summary lists it. Poor scans and handwritten invoices are the usual ones.

Can it pay our suppliers?

It lines up the payment run in your accounting system. A person approves the run before anything is paid, unless you decide otherwise.

How long does a first build take?

First builds typically ship within a few weeks. We run it alongside your team until it’s trusted, then hand it over.
