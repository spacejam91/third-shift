What we take off your plate

Repetitive work that lives in email, spreadsheets and the gaps between your systems. If someone on your team re-keys the same data every week, that’s the job.

  • Supplier invoices and payables

    Invoices arrive by email and vendor portals, get matched to purchase orders, posted to your accounting system, and queued for payment. Anything that doesn’t match waits for a person.

  • Lead intake and follow-up

    Website, email and form inquiries land in your CRM with a reply out within minutes. Open quotes get chased on a schedule, so nobody has to remember to.

  • Reporting

    The numbers you look at every Monday, pulled from the systems they live in and assembled Sunday night, instead of being built by hand that morning.

  • The process one person carries in their head

    The spreadsheet, the checklist, the Friday routine only Dave knows how to do. Written down, built out, and running on a schedule with a log of what it did.

How it starts

No discovery retainer, no slide deck. One call, one page, one build.

  1. A 20-minute call

    You walk through what eats up your team’s week. We tell you plainly what’s worth automating and what isn’t.

  2. A one-page plan

    The first automation, what it connects to, a fixed price and a delivery date. You decide from there.

  3. Build, test, hand over

    We build it, run it alongside your team until it’s trusted, then hand it over with monitoring and a log of every run. First builds typically ship within a few weeks.
